# Venture Pulse — Hackathon Judging Walkthrough & Evaluation Guide
**A 3-Minute End-to-End Evaluation Script for Judges & Evaluators**

Welcome, judges! This guide takes you through the core workflow of **Venture Pulse** in under 3 minutes, highlighting how our multi-agent council uses Qloo’s Taste Graph to outperform generic AI.

---

### Step 1: Instant Studio Intake (30 Seconds)
1. Open the live deployment URL.
2. In the top bar of the **Studio Wizard**, click on one of the **Sample Studio Presets**:
   - ⚡ **Preset 1: "Neon Void Studio"** (Cyberpunk Indie Game Studio)
   - 🌿 **Preset 2: "Botanical Frequencies"** (Lo-Fi Wellness Podcast)
   - 📷 **Preset 3: "Analog Nostalgia"** (35mm Film & Vintage Vinyl Creator)
3. Notice how the wizard auto-populates the creator’s artistic anchors across music, cinema, and visual aesthetics, plus target collab domains (Apparel, Food/Bev, Tech, Living).
4. Click **"Launch Multi-Agent Council Debate"**.

---

### Step 2: Observe the Live Multi-Agent Debate (45 Seconds)
1. Watch the **Council Terminal** stream thoughts and tool invocations in real time:
   - 🕵️ **Cultural Analyst:** Calls `qloo_taste_explorer` to resolve anchors and extract non-obvious entity affinity scores.
   - 📈 **Brand Strategist:** Intervenes, calling `market_feasibility_validator` to evaluate deal sizing, sponsor budget brackets, and brand safety.
   - 🎨 **Creative Director:** Calls `creative_concept_synthesizer` to design the physical limited-edition product drop, materials, and packaging spec.
2. Observe how the agents reach consensus when the **Cultural Synergy Score** exceeds 85%.

---

### Step 3: Explore the Brand CRM Kanban Board (45 Seconds)
1. Once the debate finishes, you are taken to the **Actionable Brand CRM Board**.
2. Notice the cards in the **Discovered** and **Vetted** columns:
   - Each card displays the target brand, domain badge, and empirical affinity multiplier (e.g., `⚡ 7.8x Affinity`).
   - Cards display estimated deal sizing tiers (e.g. `$5k-$20k Mid Tier`).
3. Click on any card to open the **Poster & Pitch Inspector**:
   - Inspect the **Physical Collab Concept** (packaging spec, retail price, limited drop unit count).
   - Review the **Personalized Outreach Pitch** containing the data-backed Qloo citation.
4. Try **dragging a card** to the **"Pitch Ready"** or **"Dispatched"** column (state persists across browser refreshes).

---

### Step 4: Test the "Proof of Difference" Engine (30 Seconds)
1. Inside any card or at the top of the Kanban board, click **"Compare vs. Vanilla LLM"**.
2. A side-by-side comparison modal opens:
   - **Left Panel (Vanilla AI):** Shows what an ungrounded LLM generates (generic logo t-shirts, coffee mugs, cliché VPN sponsors).
   - **Right Panel (Venture Pulse):** Shows the empirical Qloo entity path and authentic subcultural product collaboration.
3. This directly demonstrates the hackathon criteria: *cultural intelligence as the true differentiator*.

---

### Step 5: Test Webhook Dispatch & Export (30 Seconds)
1. On any "Pitch Ready" card, click **"Dispatch to CRM"**.
2. The agent executes `crm_webhook_dispatcher`, simulating instant synchronization to HubSpot, Notion, or your custom webhook URL with a verified timestamp.
3. Click **"Export Dossier"** at the top right to download a formatted Markdown/JSON dossier of the entire collaboration strategy.
